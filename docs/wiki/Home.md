# ServiceNow Platform MCP

Use your AI app to ask questions about ServiceNow. You can also prepare changes
when your administrator enables the right tools.

## Find your way

- New to the connection? Start with [Getting Started](Getting-Started.md).
- Working with tickets or requests? See [ITSM Work](ITSM-Work.md).
- Investigating or developing an instance? See [Instance Development](Instance-Development.md).
- Setting up access? See [Configuration](Configuration.md) and [Safety and Policy](Safety-and-Policy.md).

For the available tools, see [Tool Packages](Tool-Packages.md) and
[Tool Reference](Tool-Reference.md). For maintainers, see [Development](Development.md),
[Architecture](Architecture.md), and [Telemetry](Telemetry.md).

Your ServiceNow account determines which records you can access. Check important
results in ServiceNow before acting on them.
