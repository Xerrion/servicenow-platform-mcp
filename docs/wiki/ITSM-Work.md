# Work with tickets and requests

Ask your AI app to find records, show their details, or summarize a history.
Your account needs permission to read the information.

## Questions to try

Replace the example values with ones from your instance.

- "Show my active incidents. Include the number, priority, and short description."
- "Show recent updates for INC0012345. Include dates and authors."
- "Show the submitted answers for RITM0012345."
- "Count open incidents by assignment group. Show the filters used."

Include a time period and a result limit when you need a short list. Check the
filters and returned records before using a summary in a decision.

## Prepare a change

Changes need write tools and ServiceNow permission. Ask the app to read the
current record and show a preview. Review the target and the proposed values.
Tell the app to wait for your approval before applying the preview.

The server does not verify that a person approved the change. A preview can be
applied by the app using its token. See [Safety and Policy](Safety-and-Policy.md)
for the limits and what to do if a result is uncertain.
