# Getting started

## Connect

Ask your administrator for your ServiceNow address and OAuth client ID. Then
follow the [installation guide](https://github.com/Xerrion/servicenow-platform-mcp/blob/main/INSTALL.md)
to add the server to your AI app.

You need a ServiceNow account with access to the records you want to use.
The first request may open a browser for sign in. Use the browser on the
computer that runs the server.

## Try a question

Ask your AI app:

> Show me one active incident I can access. Include its number and short description.

Open the result in ServiceNow to check it. If you cannot read incidents, choose
another table your account can access. A list of tools confirms that the server
started; a successful record read also checks the connection and your access to
that record.
